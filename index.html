1. Embassies & Diplomatic Missions

+
  • French Embassy in Singapore — best for French nationals; admin, culture, economic
  • EU Delegation to Singapore — policy, trade, political affairs
  • ASEAN Member State Embassies — cultural diplomacy, admin roles
  • Swiss Embassy — cross-border cooperation themes
  • Other European Embassies (DE, IT, ES, BE, NL) — short internships for EU citizens

2. Intergovernmental & International Organisations

+
  • Asia–Europe Foundation (ASEF) — EU–Asia relations, diplomacy
  • IATA – Asia-Pacific Office — policy, regulatory affairs
  • OECD Singapore Centre — economic/Asia-policy analysis
  • UN Agencies in SG — UNHCR, UNDP regional links

3. Think Tanks & Research Centres

+
  • RSIS — geopolitics, security, diplomacy research
  • ISEAS–Yusof Ishak Institute — SEA geopolitics, trade
  • Asia Competitiveness Institute — economics, policy, global affairs
  • Future Issues & Global Governance Labs — international governance

4. NGOs & Civil Society Organisations

+
  • Singapore International Foundation — intercultural programmes
  • HOME — migration, rights advocacy
  • AWARE — gender, policy analysis
  • Red Cross / Mercy Relief — humanitarian operations

5. Trade & Economic Agencies

+
  • Business France (Singapore) — trade missions, market entry
  • Enterprise Europe Network Asia-Pacific — EU–Asia trade cooperation
  • Swiss Global Enterprise / EU Trade Offices — economic diplomacy
  • Singapore Tourism Board (STB) — tourism strategy

6. MNCs (Public Affairs & Government Relations)

+
  • Public Affairs / Geopolitical Risk Teams in:
    Airbus, Thales, Safran (French strategic sectors)
    TotalEnergies, Engie (energy + regulatory)
    BNP Paribas, Société Générale (EU–Asia regulatory)
    Google, Meta, Amazon (public policy)
  • Consulting firms: EY, Deloitte, KPMG, PwC, McKinsey, BCG, Bain

7. Practical Reminders

+
  • Ensure host sponsors Training Employment Pass
  • Target Europe–Asia, trade, diplomatic themes to leverage French background
  • Embassies & int'l orgs: cold outreach needed — low visibility, high potential
